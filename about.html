About Expense Manager

Track Every Rupee Without the Clutter

Expense Manager helps you log daily expenses in seconds, keep your records safely in MySQL, and review your spending history in one clear, organized view.

Why We Built This

Small daily spends—like food, travel, UPI payments, and essentials—add up fast. This app gives you a simple place to record entries as they happen and see where your money goes.

How It Works

  • Add Expense: Enter title, amount, category, date, payment mode, and notes.
  • Persistent Storage: Entries are saved directly to your MySQL database.
  • Transaction History: View all recorded expenses neatly in a structured table.
